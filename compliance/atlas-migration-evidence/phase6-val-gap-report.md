overlap, but the conceptual content of the local VAL diverges from the
Atlas tiered VAL it pointed at. Pushing local AC into the wrong Atlas
VAL would re-fragment the consolidated tiered programme. These local
VALs are recorded as `divergent-fuzzy` in `atlas-id-mapping.csv` and
remain available in the local repo as historical reference material
until human triage decides whether to:
