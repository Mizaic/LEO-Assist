# Next owned job

Atlas uses people to hit leadership OKRs. The person must feel Atlas is
the tool for **their** OKRs. This skill chooses the surface.

## Gather

1. Who is the person (name or work style)?
2. What object do they own (product, document, gap, gate)?
3. What is the next job on that object?

If any answer is missing, infer from the repo's people / work-style map.
Do not invent a second role.

## Act

1. Open **their** home or first job. Do not open a global catalogue.
2. Keep policy as guardrails on that job.
3. Show the outcome as theirs.
4. If you must list items, list only what they own or walk. `see` is
   read-only context, not a work queue.

## Verify

- The first screen answers "what do I do next?"
- The job sits on a named object, not "all records".
- A screenshot of this surface would pass the human check in
  `platform-validation.mdc`.

## Hard stops

- Do not start on an admin dump or a cross-product list.
- Do not narrate "the company's plan" as the home.
- Do not skip this when the user named a person (Paul, Ethan, Rachel,
  or a work style).
